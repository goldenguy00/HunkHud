# HUNK
- Adds HUNK from the Resident Evil franchise
- Has a handful of unlockable skins/knife skins and is fully multiplayer compatible
- Comes with a unique side objective and a ton of unique weapons to obtain (permanently)
- Configurable stats and a toooon of other things to mess around with
- Full Risk of Options support for all configuration options
- Highly customizable with tons of different loadout options to change the way you play the game

# Skills

## Main Passive

[![](https://i.postimg.cc/wxQsgFnS/image.png)]()

Every stage an enemy will get infected with a Virus, powering it up.

Once defeated, you will obtain a virus sample that can be turned into an Umbrella Terminal to exchange it for a keycard. Keycards can be used to open the corresponding weapon cases which you'll find on each stage.

[![](https://i.postimg.cc/LHWW33QK/image.png)]()

On stage 1, the Virus will always be a weaker strain of the G-Virus, and there will always be only one weapon case. After stage 1, there will be two cases per stage and a variety of different viruses, listed below.


### G-Virus

1 infected monster will mutate over time, up to 5 times max, gaining more power each time. Falling below 50% health immediately mutates, healing 25% max health.

[![](https://i.postimg.cc/M65XMXKL/image.png)]()

Once the host is killed, the G-Young parasite jumps out. Killing this will drop the G-Virus Sample.

[![](https://i.postimg.cc/sgzvGWSt/image.png)]()


### T-Virus

7 infected monsters will resist death two times, healing to full the first time and half health the second time. T-Virus Sample drops when the last monster is killed.

[![](https://i.postimg.cc/pLFjbxvB/image.png)]()


### C-Virus

4 infected monsters will resist death once, entering a frenzy and exploding after a few seconds. C-Virus Sample drops when the last monster explodes.

[![](https://i.postimg.cc/4yY0XnBd/image.png)]()


### Uroboros

1 monster will be infected with the Uroboros virus, granting it the power to dodge 7 instances of damage before stunning itself and recharging this ability. Playing around the window of vulnerability is key. Killing this monster drops the Uroboros Sample.

[![](https://i.postimg.cc/3JBtzXKB/image.png)]()

# Unique Item Interactions
Since cooldown items do almost nothing on HUNK, most of them have unique interactions that play around his ammo system instead


Backup Magazine - +1 fake bullet every time you complete a reload

Bandolier - Gives you half a mag of fake bullets on pickup

Alien Head - Increased ammo on pickup, same scaling and nerfed if Green Alien Head is installed

Brainstalks - Shots don't consume ammo while active

Lysate Cell - +1 fake bullet every time you swap to a new gun

Purity - Double magazine capacity (consumption remains the same)


FAKE BULLETS can be fired without consuming any actual ammo, but are all lost when you reload or swap weapons

## Recommended Mods

Below are mods recommended to get the full HUNK experience

[USS Squad](https://thunderstore.io/package/VCR/USS_Squad/) - A pack of unique, high quality skins featuring other Resident Evil characters

[OutOfBoundsItemsFix](https://thunderstore.io/package/public_ParticleSystem/OutOfBoundsItemsFix/) - Prevents your Samples from falling off the map

[GupAttackTelegraph](https://thunderstore.io/package/public_ParticleSystem/GupAttackTelegraph/) - Helps counter Gup attacks

[Catacombs](https://thunderstore.io/package/viliger/Catacombs/) - Indoors stage that fits really well with HUNK's close-up camera

[MoreSacrificeInteractables](https://thunderstore.io/package/Moffein/MoreSacrificeInteractables/) - More ammo pickups when playing with Sacrifice on

[GreenAlienHead](https://thunderstore.io/package/duckduckgreyduck/GreenAlienHead/) - Makes Alien Head more accessible, which helps when gathering ammo

[Jacket](https://thunderstore.io/package/poopchudgamer422/Jacket/) - Features Jacket from Payday 2 for a more streamlined gameplay experience with HUNK's kit, especially good for alternate gamemodes like Simulacrum
